Alexander Garavito Shtefan

PhD in Political Science

I hold a PhD in Political Science from the University of Guadalajara. My work examines the history of political ideas, transitions and social movements, and contemporary transformations related to technology and globalization. I have worked in research, university teaching, editing and public policy advising.
